Twelve-week build path

Reading repos does not make you good at this. Shipping one system, measuring it, and training on its traces does.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Write the loop by hand

    An LLM API, three tools and a while-loop. Then read mini-swe-agent and smolagents and compare.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Rebuild it in three frameworks

    LangGraph, the OpenAI Agents SDK and Pydantic AI. Note what each abstraction costs and what it buys.

  3. Week 5

    Turn your services into tools

    Expose ASR, TTS and translation as MCP servers. Add one A2A agent.

  4. Weeks 6–7

    Make it survive failure

    Run the loop inside Temporal, kill workers mid-task and prove it resumes. Put a gateway with fallbacks in front of the models.

  5. Week 8

    Trace every step

    OpenTelemetry into Langfuse or Phoenix. Cost and latency per step, not per request.

  6. Week 9

    Measure before tuning

    A tau2-bench style eval for your own domain, built in Inspect. Grade trajectories, not only answers.

  7. Week 10

    Serve it yourself

    Host an open model on SGLang and measure prefix-cache hit rate on real agent traces.

  8. Weeks 11–12

    Train on your own traces

    SFT a small model with TRL, then multi-turn GRPO with verl or SkyRL, using your eval as the reward.
